# The hive itself
## Why this branch exists
The meta, taken as tasks: the hive builds its tools, its judges, its organization and this knowledge (docs/balance.md).
## What matters here
Proof required: the tool's tests and the common judge's meta-tests, then review by a guardian or the council; the
council merges: nothing here is integrated automatically (categories.yaml).
## What you can ignore here
The rules and their values (RULES.md, bee.yaml) change not through a task but through a proposal (R10); the
commandments, through the human council alone (11).
## Children
- `projects/ruche/ruche`: the registry: the bee tool, the site, the common judge, the schemas, the workflows, the docs
