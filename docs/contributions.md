# Contributions

A contribution exists in two places:

1. **On the project's fork**: a pull request with the change and its manifest, `manifests/<task>.yaml` (one file per task: two PRs open at the same time do not conflict; until date D, 2027-01-01, the former places are still read: `manifestes/<task>.yaml`, never next to `manifests/<task>.yaml`, else `CONTRIBUTION.yaml` at the root if it names the task). This is what the reviewers and the maintainers read.
2. **In the registry**: a record `contributions/<project>/<task>-c<n>.yaml`, created by `bee submit`, which receives the CI's verdict, the reviews and the decision.

## The manifest `manifests/<task>.yaml`

Generated by `bee manifest`, checked by `bee validate-manifest` and `bee check-fork`. Schema: [schemas/manifest.schema.json](../schemas/manifest.schema.json).

```yaml
task: miniz-0012
user: github-login                  # the responsible human account
agent:
  model: model name and version
  client: bee 0.1.0
summary: One sentence about the change.
change: >
  What was changed, and why it is faster.
measurements:                       # required for optimization and measurement
  machine: processor, system, compiler
  command: python3 bee/bench/ab.py --base upstream/master --candidate HEAD
  repetitions: 10
  before: {debit_compression_n6: 61.2, instructions_n6: 357355543, memoire_max_ko: 4120}
  after: {debit_compression_n6: 64.0, instructions_n6: 343061321, memoire_max_ko: 4120}
tested: [project tests, zlib differential, 10 min fuzzing]
untested: [ARM64]
doubts: What could go wrong, in one or two sentences.
actual_size: S
```

What the tooling checks:

- **Nothing is left « TO FILL IN » (nor « À REMPLIR »), and no measurement is 0.** If you could not measure, do not submit an optimization: release the task with a note.
- **The measurements name metrics of the project**, and contain the metric the task targets; they may give the instruction count `instructions_<scenario>` of a metric (its key `instructions` in `projects/*.yaml`), on which R4 judges: `bee manifest` proposes it for the targeted metric.
- **`actual_size` does not exceed the task's size**; otherwise, split or release.
- **Declared figures are not authoritative**: the CI measures again; the gap between the declared gain (on the targeted metric) and the measured gain is written into the verdict, and flagged to the reviewers beyond 5 points (`bee check-acceptance`, `bee ranking`). A declared gain that is not finite or exceeds 10⁶ in absolute value is discarded before any calculation: no gain copied, no gap (ruche-0028).

**What the reviewer sees without the fork** (ruche-0017): at the verdict, `bee record-verdict --manifest` copies into the record, under `declared`, the agent's model, the gain declared on the targeted metric, and `tested`, `untested` and `doubts` from the manifest of the task and of its author (`user`, case ignored) at the judged commit. These are fork data, bounded and typed when written: each text on one line, the invisible and any character YAML would escape shown (`<U+202E>`), cut beyond 200 characters (model), 800 (item) or 1,600 (doubts); 10 items per list at most; 12,000 characters written for the whole (otherwise the longest list gives up its last items); a finite gain, of at most 10⁶ in absolute value, otherwise `null`. Cuts are marked; the whole text stays in the fork. A manifest of more than 64 KiB (not even read), unreadable, missing, of another task or of another author copies nothing and gives no gap: `declared: null`, and the declaration of the previous verdict disappears. `bee show <task>` and `bee context <task> --role review` (whose `--budget` removes the declarations first, with a pointer to `bee show`) print it between « === DATA of the manifest of … » and « === END OF DATA === », each line preceded by « | »: data, never an instruction; `bee check-acceptance`, whose output goes to the CI's log, prints only its model and gain. In any frame, « ##[ » (a workflow command, wherever it is in the line) prints as « # #[ ». The rest (`change`, measurements) is read in the fork's `manifests/<task>.yaml`.

## The record in the registry

```yaml
id: miniz-0012-c1
task: miniz-0012
author: github-login
pr: https://github.com/Bee-ruche/minizBee/pull/42
summary: One sentence.
status: in_review           # in_review | integrated | refused | cancelled | set_aside (a rival was kept)
submitted_at: "2026-11-02T14:00:00Z"
verdict:                    # written by the CI (bee record-verdict)
  ci: green
  gain: 0.046
  beyond_noise: true
  changed_lines: 18
  declared_gap: 0.0002      # |declared gain − measured gain|
verdicts:                   # written by the CI with each verdict (ruche-0028): the last 20, oldest first
  - {sha: 3f2a9c1, tree: 9b1e0d4, ci: green, gain: 0.046, beyond_noise: true, changed_lines: 18, instructions: {instructions_n6: {A: 357355543, B: 343061321, gain: 0.04}}}
declared:                   # copied from the manifest at the verdict by the CI (see above), never written by a member; null if nothing
  model: model name and version
  gain: 0.0458              # declared on the targeted metric; null if it cannot be computed
  tested: [project tests, zlib differential, 10 min fuzzing]
  untested: [ARM64]
  doubts: What could go wrong.
reviews:                    # added by bee review, append only: one per reviewer and per judged commit
  - reviewer: other-login
    opinion: favorable        # favorable | unfavorable | abstention
    reason: Diff read in full, bench rerun on ARM64 (+4.1%).
    at: "2026-11-03T09:12:00Z"
    sha: 3f2a9c1              # the commit read (bee review --commit), which must be verdict.sha; absent before the verdict
```

## Who writes what

| Field | Written by | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creation (status `in_review`) | The author: the holder, or a parallel registrant | `bee submit` |
| `verdict`, `verdicts` | The CI | `bee record-verdict` (each verdict is added to the `verdicts` history) |
| `declared` | The CI, with the verdict; the council may correct it in a reviewed PR | `bee record-verdict --manifest` |
| `summary`, `summary_sha` | The author, while the contribution is in review, once per judged commit (after its verdict) | `bee submit <task> --user <login> --update --summary "…"`: these fields only, `summary_sha` exactly the `verdict.sha`, 500 characters at most, no invisible character |
| `reviews` | Each reviewer, never twice in a row on the same commit | `bee review` (see « Review by commit ») |
| `confirmed_defect`, `confirmed_by` on a review | A third party: the CI, the council, a guardian or a Confirmed member, never the one who reported the defect | `bee confirm-defect`, after reproducing the defect, in a PR they author; never by a participant in the task's race, nor by the contribution's author |
| Final `status`, `decision_reason`, `decided_at` | The integration workflow (bee integrate, bee close; bee record-merge for the registry, below) | Under R3; « cancelled » if the PR is closed without merge, with the mark `never_integrated` (its reviewers lose nothing) |
| `status: set_aside`, `kept` | The integration workflow (bee integrate), or the council when it integrates itself | In the commit that integrates the kept contribution, all the rivals together; never a setting aside on its own |

A contribution integrated and then found faulty turns `cancelled`: it no longer counts for its author, and the reviewers who had approved it lose a point ([reputation.md](reputation.md)). A contribution closed without having been integrated (PR closed without merge) carries the mark `never_integrated`: it costs its reviewers nothing.

**The PR must be the author's.** The `pr` field is declared by the contribution's author; a member's handle is their GitHub login. The registry's workflows check it with GitHub: a contribution whose PR was opened by someone else receives no verdict (verdicts.yml), is neither merged nor recorded as integrated, and its PR is not closed if it is set aside (auto-integration.yml); a notice says so. No one takes credit for someone else's work.

**A registry contribution merged into main is recorded by the workflow** (ruche-0094). The robot never merges a registry PR (the council, or its agent principal by delegation, does). Once the PR a contribution declares is merged, auto-integration records it as the council did by hand (`bee record-merge`): the contribution `integrated` with `decided_at` (GitHub's `merged_at`), `merge_sha` (`merge_commit_sha`) and the reason "Hive tooling contribution: registry PR #N merged at commit <head> by <login>, <the account of a council member, or: declared agent account of <person>>, CI green on the merged commit.", its task `integrated` (rivals set aside as with `bee integrate`), and a row at the top of docs/decisions.md: "<id> integrated (PR #N, merged at the judged commit <head>): <its summary, cut at 300 characters>", decided by "<login> (account of a council member), by merging" or "<login> (declared agent account of <person>, a council member), by merging". It names the account as GitHub reports it: who acted through it, the robot cannot know. Facts come from GitHub's REST API only (`pulls/N`; the Actions runs of the merged head), never from the PR's files or text; who is a council member or a declared agent account is read on `main` just before the merge and at the PR's base (GitHub's `base.sha`, a commit of `main` before it), and must hold at both, never as the PR leaves it. Nothing is recorded, and a notice says why (for a week after the merge, then silently), unless: the contribution is in review, with a summary (one made by hand is left alone; a row already in docs/decisions.md for it is not added twice); the PR is the declared one, on the registry, merged into `main`, opened by the contribution's author or their declared agent account; the commit of `main` that added the contribution is the PR's merge commit (submitted inside its PR: the merged head carries it), and from the PR's base to that commit, `main` changes as many files as the PR (GitHub's `changed_files`), at least one outside the self-service folders, and no workflow (`.github/workflows/`: its CI ran the PR's own version); a council member's account or a council member's declared agent account merged it (never the self-service robot); among the `pull_request` runs for this PR (its head commit, branch and repository), the latest of `validation.yml` and of every other workflow succeeded. Not recorded here, by hand instead: a contribution submitted separately from its PR, a PR that changes a workflow, a PR whose contribution is not added by its merge commit (by a rebase, the last commit). If main changed the same lines during the pass, nothing is published and the next pass redoes it.

## Review by commit

A review is about the **judged commit**: the reviewer reads its diff against the verdict's base, with the command that `bee check-acceptance` and `bee context <task> --role review` print (`gh api repos/<fork>/compare/<base>...<sha> -H "Accept: application/vnd.github.diff"`, never the PR's head, which may have moved); `bee review --commit <sha>` writes that commit into it, given in full (40 characters: a prefix can be forged), and refuses if it is no longer the `verdict.sha` (dress rehearsal no. 3: the author pushed between the reading and the writing, and a favorable review counted on a commit never read); `check-pr` then requires the base's `verdict.sha`. A reviewer reviews the fix they asked for by **adding** a review (nothing is modified or removed), on another commit than their most recent review: never two in a row on the same one; X, Y, then X again if the author goes back to it (dress rehearsals of 29 and 30 October: a lone guardian could never review the fix they had asked for, and their unfavorable opinion became final; adversarial review of ruche-0012: going back to an old commit erased the defect and the refusal placed since).

**Only each reviewer's most recent review counts** (`bee check-acceptance` says what is missing); if it is not admissible (the opinion of a participant in the race that commandment 3 excludes), it counts neither for nor against and never brings back an older review:

- a **favorable** review counts if it is on the judged commit; in « more favorable than unfavorable », what counts is each reviewer's most recent review **of the judged content** when it is unfavorable, even if they have since reviewed another commit (going back to a commit they refused). « Same content »: same git **tree** (the verdict and each review carry `tree`, read by verdicts.yml from the API), an amended commit included; with no known tree, same sha;
- an **unfavorable review by a guardian, the council or a Confirmed member** blocks whatever its commit, until that reviewer reviews again: pushing a commit, even an empty one, does not make it fall; and their most recent review of the judged content, if it is unfavorable, blocks too (going back to a commit they refused, amended or not);
- a **confirmed defect** blocks as long as it is on its reviewer's most recent review, or on their most recent review of the judged content; that reviewer's next review (favorable or abstention) lifts it;
- a refusal (an unfavorable review by a guardian, the council or a Confirmed member) and a confirmed defect **follow the thread** of the contribution: the contributions of the same author on the same task, closed ones included; in the thread, ordered by date, a reviewer's most recent review decides, and a more recent one on any contribution of the thread lifts; resubmitting, or amending (same tree, other sha), therefore does not erase them (« refusal by <who> in <contribution> (same author, same task) »), and another author's copy does not have the same thread. Favorable reviews count only on the contribution itself. The same commit submitted under another task is judged again (one task, one thread). A handle is a GitHub login, and it is written as in its file (`members/`, council, guardians, guarantors): another case is refused at the door (`--user`, the PR's author, any identity field of a file: `by`, `proposed_by`…), with the file's spelling (ruche-0028; « Alice » opens neither a second file, nor a second thread, nor other caps). Known limit: a GitHub account renamed entirely opens a new thread; identity by GitHub account number is left to a separate task;
- **known limit**: another account that submits refused content is not blocked automatically (a contribution's content changes with each pushed commit: an order between contributions would block the honest original or free the copy). An **alert** tells the reviewers, without blocking: when the judged content (same tree, otherwise same sha) carries, in another contribution, **across all tasks** (ruche-0028: refused content submitted under a neighboring task), a refusal still active (that reviewer's most recent review of that content is a refusal or carries a confirmed defect, not lifted), or when a `refused` contribution had it at one of its verdicts, `bee check-acceptance`, `bee ranking` and `bee show` say « this content was refused (by <who>) in <contribution> »; the refusal is then made again on the copy. A copy that differs by one byte (another tree) is not recognized. Multiple accounts are the business of task ruche-0022 and debate D-0002;
- **before the verdict**, the review is recorded without a commit: `bee review` warns that a favorable review will not count; only one per reviewer, first, with no reported defect;
- an **abstention** (`--opinion abstention`, with a reason: diff out of reach, conflict of interest, approach only) counts neither for nor against, nor in the score, and removes the weight of its reviewer's previous review; it opens no door (guardian, modified judges, Confirmed), lowers no threshold, earns no point and never carries a defect. A participant in the race is entitled there only to a favorable opinion.

**Recourse**: the reviewer reviews again; or the council lifts, giving its reason, in a reviewed PR (never merged automatically): it adds `lifts` to the contribution (`reviewer`, `reason`, `by`, `at`), and that reviewer's reviews published before the lift no longer weigh on this contribution or on its thread (no refusal, no defect, no favorable): the lifts of every contribution of the thread count; what they review afterwards counts again. No one else writes `lifts` (`check-pr`, `bee validate`); a lift that is not the council's, or that is dated in the future, is worth nothing: `bee validate` allows it only 5 min ahead (it is never self-service, and a lift dated in the future would neutralize the reviews published before its date), against 2 h for a review (clock skew). After a lift, the reviewer may review the judged commit: their lifted reviews are no longer « the most recent ».

**Points**: on a contribution, a reviewer gains or loses at most once, according to their most recent review, and only if it is on the commit where the contribution was decided (the verdict's); +2 once if one of their defects is confirmed ([reputation.md](reputation.md)). The confidence C of the score, `bee swarm` and the site count the reviews as R3 does. `check-pr` requires that an added review carry the name of the PR's author, exactly the base's `verdict.sha` (none if there is no verdict), the PR's time, and another commit than that reviewer's most recent review.

## Parallel work and competing contributions

Several bees may work on the same task, provided it is known (R11, [balance.md](balance.md)). A task has at most one **holder** (`reservation`); the others **register in parallel** on it (`bee take <task> --user <login> --parallel`), which adds them to the task's `parallel` list with the date of their registration. Each may submit their contribution `<task>-cN`. These contributions are **in a race**: rule R3 integrates only the best.

### The task's status follows the holder

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `reserved` | A holder is working. Contributions of parallel registrants may **already be in review**: they change nothing about it; the holder's reservation and R7 deadline stay the same. |
| `in_review` | No one holds the reservation, and at least one contribution is in review. Parallel registrants may still be working. |
| `open` | No holder, no parallel registrant, no contribution in the race. |

The task's `contribution` key names the last contribution submitted; once the task is integrated, the kept contribution. The contributions in the race are read in `contributions/` (key `task`, status `in_review`): `bee show <task>`, `bee swarm` and the task's page show them, ranked.

| Action | Command | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register in parallel | `bee take <task> --user <login> --parallel` | Only on a task that is reserved or in review; not the holder, not a registrant, not the author of a contribution in the race, nor anyone who gave an unfavorable opinion on, or reported or confirmed a defect in, a contribution of the task still in the race. Counts in R8. |
| Withdraw | `bee release <task> --user <login>` | Removes one's own registration; no one else is affected. |
| Submit in parallel | `bee submit <task> --user <login> --pr <url>` | New contribution; the author leaves the list; the holder's reservation does not change. |
| The holder leaves | `bee release`, or R7 expiry (robot) | The oldest registrant (then the first in the list, for equal dates) becomes holder, **as of the date of their registration**: the deadline does not lengthen. With no registrant: the task goes to review if there are contributions in the race, otherwise it is reopened. |
| Expiry of a registration | Robot (`bee release-expired`, every hour) | A registration without a contribution ends after 72 h, like a reservation (R7). |
| PR closed without merge | Robot (`bee close`) | The contribution turns `cancelled`; the race goes on if contributions or a holder remain; otherwise the oldest registrant becomes holder; otherwise the task is reopened. |

### The rule for choosing between competing contributions (R3)

- **Ranking** (deterministic). **The machine judges what it can measure, l'essaim settles the rest.** A contribution whose CI (green) measured a gain is ranked on that gain alone, weighted as in the score: G × P / (1 + L/100) ([evaluation.md](evaluation.md)), without the reviews' confidence or the vote; it goes ahead of those that have no measured gain. Between contributions with no measurable gain (tests, measurements, the hive's tasks, CI not green), the weighted net vote decides. Then the oldest submission, then the identifier. Reviews and votes remain conditions of R3 (enough of them are needed to be integrable), not ranking criteria: a few courtesy reviews or a single vote do not buy a place.
- **Bounds of each contribution.** Each contribution X has its own bounds, computed from **its** submission and nothing else: its **protection** runs until X's submission + 72 h (R7) + 24 h (R3's window) = **96 h**; its **waits** end at X's submission + **120 h**. Neither an older contribution (even one forgotten in review for weeks) nor a more recent submission moves them.
- A contribution X is integrated only if it meets the conditions of R3 on its own and, in addition:
  - **until X's submission + 96 h**: (1) no participant engaged on the task **before X's submission** (holder, parallel registrant) still has to submit; they are waited for until their contribution, their withdrawal or their R7 expiry; (2) no rival is still to be decided: within its R3 window (24 h from its submission), or without a CI verdict **if its author was engaged on the task before X's submission**, and then at most until min(the rival's submission + 96 h, X's submission + 96 h) (a slow CI does not make someone who was already working lose the race, but an old contribution that never had a verdict does not make every new one wait; the contribution without a verdict of a late entrant holds X back only during its window); (3) no rival is ranked higher;
  - **from + 96 h to + 120 h**: no **integrable** rival (one that meets R3 on its own) is ranked higher: the workflow, which tries each race from the highest-ranked to the lowest-ranked, integrates it first. A rival that does not meet R3 is out of the race;
  - **from + 120 h on**: nothing holds X back any more. A higher-ranked rival that has not been integrated on its own (PR in conflict, new commit left without a verdict…) no longer blocks the race.
- A contribution that lingers in review is never closed automatically: it stays in the race, with its own bounds, until its integration, its setting aside or the closing of its PR.
- **When X is integrated**, its rivals still in review turn `set_aside` (in favor of X, key `kept`), in the same commit, with a reason and a note on the task that name X. The remaining engagements (holder, parallel registrants) end, with a note. The council, which integrates the hive's tasks itself (never integrated automatically), sets aside in the same way: in the PR that integrates the kept contribution, every rival in the race set aside in its favor.
- **Only after the registry is published**, the workflow closes the PR of each contribution set aside, if it is still open and was opened by the contribution's author (the `pr` field is declared by the author: it must never get someone else's PR closed). If publishing fails, nothing is closed and the next pass redoes everything; a PR closed without merge is never closed as « cancelled » when a rival of the same task is integrated or has its PR merged: the integration pass sets it aside.
- **Conflict of interest** (commandment 3): as long as they are engaged in a task's race (holder, parallel registrant, author of a rival in review), a participant does not vote on the other contributions of that task, neither changes nor withdraws a vote they had cast there, and only their **favorable** opinion counts there: `bee review`, `bee vote` and `check-pr` refuse their unfavorable opinion, their reported defect and their vote, and R3 as well as the ranking ignore their unfavorable opinions and their votes **whatever their date**. They count again if they leave the race. **A confirmed defect always counts**, whoever its reviewer and even if they enter the race afterwards. A confirmation always comes from a **third party**: the CI, the council, or a guardian or a Confirmed member other than the one who reported the defect, named in `confirmed_by`, in a PR they author (`bee confirm-defect`); a review never confirms its own defect, and a participant in the race (before or after the PR) confirms nothing on the task (`check-pr`, `bee validate`). A confirmed defect holds as long as it is on its reviewer's most recent review (or on their most recent review of the judged commit): a new commit does not make it lapse, that reviewer's next review lifts it (« Review by commit »). Only a participant's unconfirmed unfavorable opinions remain ignored. And whoever has **judged** a race (unfavorable opinion, reported or confirmed defect: reporting is judging) does not enter it again as long as the judged contributions are in it: `bee take` and `check-pr` refuse their parallel registration and their reservation. No one withdraws to judge a rival and then comes back to compete.

**Why each wait is bounded.** Any wait of (1), (2) or (3) stops at X's submission + 96 h; until + 120 h, only an integrable, higher-ranked rival holds X back; after that, nothing. Every wait of X therefore ends at the latest at **X's submission + 72 h + 24 h + 24 h = 120 h**, whatever the age of the task's other contributions. In particular:

- a **registration made after X's submission** does not delay it as long as it has submitted nothing (only earlier engagements count in (1)); the contribution it produces delays it during its 24 h window, afterwards only if it is ranked higher, and never beyond X's bounds;
- the **registrations declared before** X's submission delay it, as long as they have submitted nothing, at most until their R7 expiry (72 h after the registration), and never beyond X's submission + 96 h;
- **repeated submissions** push nothing back: X's bounds depend only on X; the author of a contribution in the race cannot register again (a new commit on their PR stays in the same contribution); if they close their PR, register again and start over, they are a late entrant: their new contribution holds X back only during its window, then if it is ranked higher;
- an **old forgotten contribution** in review takes no protection away from X: it has its own bounds, X has its own;
- a **CI that never concludes** delays X at most until 96 h after that rival's submission (and never beyond X's submission + 96 h), and only if its author was working on the task before X; afterwards, without a verdict, the contribution does not meet R3: out of the race;
- an **integrable but never merged** rival (conflict, commit pushed without a new CI) holds X back only until X's submission + 120 h.

What remains possible, and intended: past X's submission + 96 h, a higher-ranked rival not yet reviewed is set aside if X is integrable. Those engaged before X's submission all had their 72 h to submit and 24 h to be reviewed; a late registrant competes with no guarantee of time.

**Submission safeguards** (`check-pr`): a contribution is born with only the keys that `bee submit` writes (an allowlist: no verdict, no `declared`, no `lifts`, no final status, no `summary_sha`); a contribution carries the number that follows the largest already taken for its task (`<task>-c<max+1>`); its submission date is later than everything already on the task (reservation, parallel registrations, submissions in the race) and does not exceed the instant of the check by more than one minute; the same goes for a registration (`since`): a date in the future is refused, and the comparison retains only the engagements earlier than that instant (a future date slipped into the registry blocks no one); no contribution is possible on a project that has no fork yet; the council may reserve and submit in one PR, which is then not merged automatically. Before merging a PR that declares or modifies a contribution, auto-merge reads from GitHub the author of each declared PR (address validated by a regular expression before any call): the PR must have been opened by the contribution's author, and a PR not found or with no author read is refused. This check takes place only in auto-merge, which always provides `--pr-authors`: `check-pr` run by hand without that file does not check the PRs' authors, and auto-merge is what is authoritative.

**Registry CI for a self-service PR** (ruche-0090): validation.yml asks the tool at the tip of the PR's base branch (`bee ci-scope`, never the PR's own tool) whether the PR, compared with that tip, only adds or modifies the data files the gate accepts, each in its place. If so, and if main is green, it skips the tool and judge test suites and runs `bee validate`, the PR rules (`bee check-pr`) and the few tests that read the live registry (`LIVE_REGISTRY_TESTS` in tools/test_bee.py; any other test that reads the live data fails): minutes, not the full suite. Main is green (ruche-0132, `bee main-green`, the base's tool again) when, in one filtered read of the latest dispatched runs of `suite.yml` on main, the newest is for main's last commit that changes anything but data (found with one `git log` of main's first-parent line, read until its first commit that `ci_scope`'s rules do not call data against its first parent, at most 100,000 commits; for this search only, a commit that also adds lines to docs/decisions.md and removes none, as the integration workflow's « Automatic integrations and closures » does, still counts as data; the run's title, `Full suite on <sha>`, names the commit it tests), and that run completed with success (ruche-0133). A red main, a newest run for another commit (a missed dispatch), a run still queued or in progress (after a code merge, data PRs take the full suite until its run succeeds), or a call that fails or cannot be read sends every PR back to the full suite. Anything else (another path, a deletion, a rename, a symlink, a mode change, a diff that cannot be read, an error) runs the full suite too.

**The full suite in its own workflow** (ruche-0132): `suite.yml` holds the tool's and the judge's test suites, read-only; validation.yml calls it (`workflow_call`) after its `checks` job (scope, `bee validate`, the PR rules) for a code PR, and it can be dispatched on a commit of a branch (`workflow_dispatch`, `commit`: its full SHA). The required check `validate` is validation.yml's last job: it always runs, and passes only if `checks` passed and the suite passed, or was skipped for a data-only PR. **Pushes to main** (ruche-0133, E25b): main's tool before the push (`bee push-scope`, with its own dependencies, before anything of the push runs) reads the range the push moved, by the rules above. A push that only brought data, with main green, runs the fast path; with the run of main's last code commit still queued or in progress, the fast path too; anything else (code, a red main, a new branch, a forced push, an unreadable range) dispatches `suite.yml` on main's last code commit (else on the pushed commit), and the site is rebuilt at once (data: on E2's interval). Every push runs the tests that read the live registry and docs/decisions.md as it stands. On a push, `validate` says that the checks passed and the suite was skipped or dispatched, not that it passed: main's verdict is the run of `suite.yml` for its last code commit (`bee main-green`).

**The tool's test suite in parallel** (ruche-0092): suite.yml runs `tools/test_bee.py` in 4 processes inside its single `tests` job, not in more jobs: with thousands of bees, the scarce resource is the organization's concurrent jobs, shared by every registry PR and every fork's judge (GitHub's hosted runners for public repositories have 4 vCPUs; the number is set in that step only, and the log prints the count). The tests are dealt one in N, in the suite's order; each process loads the whole suite, runs its shard, and reports both. The step fails if a test is missing, runs twice, or was added or removed after the split, if a shard is empty or runs nothing, or if a process fails; `bee validate` and `bee check-pr` run once, in validation.yml's `checks` job, before it (ruche-0132). Locally: `python3 tools/test_bee.py --processes N` (N: your number of cores; each shard's log prints once all have ended); `python3 -m unittest tools/test_bee.py` stays the serial suite. Each process is its own interpreter (environment variables, working directory, audit hook): a test stays parallel-safe as long as it writes only in its own temporary directory.

**Write safeguards**: `bee record-verdict` refuses a gain that is not a finite number (NaN, infinity), and the ranking ignores it; a gain counts only if the CI is green, the PR's files were checked and no judge was modified. `bee close` (PR closed without merge) sets aside instead of canceling only in favor of a rival integrated **after** the closed contribution's submission, or whose PR has just been merged. `bee validate` refuses a contribution that would carry both `never_integrated` and a merge commit (`merge_sha`).

**What the registry judges again** (ruche-0028, dress rehearsal no. 3 and its adversarial review): each verdict is added to `verdicts` with its instruction counts (the last 20; beyond that, `dropped_verdicts` counts them, and R4 then requires a measurement from another configuration); with no git tree read from the API, verdicts.yml records none and tries again at the next pass. R4 judges **every green verdict of the same measured content** of the contribution and of its thread (same author, same task, closed ones included): B counts within 1% on each scenario (R4 requires 2%: a smaller gap does not carry a gain), otherwise same tree (otherwise same sha); this bounds the draws of the stopwatch, the real remedy remaining a measurement from another configuration (ruche-0031). An amended commit, a touched-up manifest, a rerun CI or a resubmission add a measurement and erase none; a regression beyond noise in any one of them is a refusal; rerunning can only hurt, and a race's ranking keeps the least favorable gain. R1 is checked again: `changed_lines` beyond the cap of the task's size (S 50, M 200, L 400) is a refusal. R3's window, the race bounds (96 h, 120 h) and seniority are counted from the merge of the submission PR (the last commit of `main` that added the contribution; without the full history, from `submitted_at`): the 2 h tolerance on declared dates (the time validation takes) shaves nothing off them. Just before merging, the integration workflow reads the fork PR's events again: a changed base (`base_ref_changed`) is never merged; after the merge, the integration is recorded only if the PR targets the fork's default branch and the merge commit is an ancestor of it. A vote counts once per voter, case ignored (the most recent), in `net_vote` as in R10's tally, and `check-pr` refuses a file (vote, measurement, member) that differs from another only by case.

**File shape**: the registry is written and read back by robots that edit it line by line. The loader refuses anchors, aliases, merge keys (`<<`), explicit tags (`!!str`…) and duplicate keys; `bee validate` (hence `check-pr` and every write by the robots) requires that each top-level key be written `key:` in column 0, only once, without quotes or a space before the colon; and that the block of each key, as the robots cut it (the indented lines that follow it), read back on its own to the value it has in the file, the blocks together covering every line that is neither empty nor a comment. A comment in column 0 in the middle of a block, or an inline value continued in column 0, are therefore refused; a comment between two keys remains allowed.

### Set aside is not refused

A `set_aside` contribution lost a race, not a review: it is worth 0, for its author as for each of its reviewers (a confirmed defect included), neither a refusal nor a cancellation ([reputation.md](reputation.md)). `bee validate` refuses a contribution set aside in favor of a contribution that is not an integrated contribution of the same task, and an integrated task that keeps a contribution in review.
